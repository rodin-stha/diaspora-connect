In short:

1. Who we are

Diaspora Connect is an app that helps Nepali workers in Israel report and track problems at work.

The app is operated by [legal name of the organisation responsible for the data], [address] ("we", "us"). It was developed by Kumo Labs.

If you have any question about your information, contact us at [privacy contact email].

2. What information we collect

Your account

  • Your mobile phone number, used to sign you in with a code sent by SMS.

Your personal details

  • Full name, date of birth and gender.
  • Your address in Israel: district, local authority, locality, neighbourhood and postal code.
  • An emergency contact: their name, how they are related to you, their phone number and email.

Your identity documents

  • Passport number and expiry date, national ID number and citizenship certificate number.
  • Photos you upload of your passport photo page and Israel visa page.

Your work

  • Your type of work, your work permit approval letter, and for caregivers, your care arrangement and host family.

Issues you report

  • What the issue is about, its title and description, and the employer or embassy concerned.
  • If you choose to add them: a photo, a voice recording, and the place where the issue happened on a map.
  • Your replies when a case worker asks you for more information.

Information from your device

  • A notification token, so we can send you updates about your issues. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Crash reports when the app stops working: the type of phone, operating system, app version and technical details of the error. These do not include your documents or reports.
  • The app's language.

Permissions

The app asks for these only when you use the feature, and you can say no:

  • Camera and photos, to add photos of documents or evidence.
  • Microphone, to record a voice note for an issue.
  • Location, to start the map at where you are, when you pin where an issue happened. We only use your location while you are pinning it, never in the background.

3. Why we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To understand, follow up on and help resolve the issues you report.
  • To verify your identity and work situation, with your consent, which you give in the app.
  • To contact you about your issues, including by notification.
  • To show announcements and emergency numbers.
  • To fix errors and keep the app working and secure.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

    • Google Maps, for the location map.

    • [hosting provider and country], where our servers and your information are stored.

5. How we protect it

  • All information sent between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Your sign-in is stored in your phone's secure storage.
  • So the app works without internet, it keeps a copy of what you last viewed on your phone. This copy is deleted when you log out.
  • Only authorised people can see your information on our side. [confirm access controls]
